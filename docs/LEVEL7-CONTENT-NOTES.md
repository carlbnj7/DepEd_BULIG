Decisions
- Text is kept as printed, including the module's own typos. The copyreading articles (G5 Act 13, G6 Acts 10–12) keep every error on purpose.
- "Background Information for Learners" pages are shown with the activity that follows them.
- G5: the Editorial Log form printed after Activity 12 (pp. 24–25) is the log Activity 8 asks for, so it is given with Activity 8.
- G6 pp. 27–28: the two model columns ("Cyber pornography", "Mental health") are printed as pictures of text; their words were typed out so they read on phones. They are the module's own content, so please review whether they suit your Grade 6 class.
- "Label each part" tasks (G4 Act 11, G6 Act 1) are one card per part with the five labels as choices.
- Decorative icons (book, newspaper and log stickers) were left out.
- No pre/post tests or answer keys exist in the module.
