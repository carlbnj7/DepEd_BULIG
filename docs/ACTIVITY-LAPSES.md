# Activity lapses: what pupils can't really do at home

**How this was checked:** I opened every published activity in Levels 1 to 7, in every grade (1,240 activity screens), as a pupil would see them. On each one I looked for six kinds of problem:

- words written for the teacher;
- games that need classroom things;
- work that can only be done on paper;
- drawing tasks with no way to draw;
- mistakes copied from the module;
- Level 1 classroom actions that don't work at home.

**Already fixed in this update:**
- **Level 1 pair and group work:** 23 activities. The pupil now practises with BULIG as a buddy. Class Demo keeps the module's pair and group wording.
- **Level 1 Lesson 9 poems:** the page screenshots are replaced by the poem player.

## 2. Level 1 classroom actions asked at home (3)

All three are in Level 1 · Lesson 2 · Post-assessment ("Listen and do", 2 steps).

| # | Module says | Problem at home |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Close the door gently, and walk back to your seat." | Pupils at home have no classroom "seat". |

## 3. Words written for the teacher, shown to the pupil (86)

The pupil reads lines like "The teacher shows a picture…", "Listen to your teacher as she reads…" or "Start when your teacher says Go."

| Level | How many | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Level 2A | 12 | "Listen to your teacher as she reads the word…", "Listen to the sounds your teacher is saying. Blend them…" |
| Level 3 | 52 | "Meet the short 'a' sound" teacher steps (one for every sound and blend), "Read and repeat" (×5), "Read more blend words" (×4), "Read the short a/e/i/o/u words", Odd Word Out (×5), Tell Me the Word (×5), Speed Read (×5) |
| Level 5 | 16 | Grade 1: "Listen carefully as your teacher reads…" (×6). Grade 2: "Teacher may read aloud" (×5). Grade 4: Bingo, spelling and listening tests. Grade 5: Noun or Verb test. Grade 6: spelling dictation. |
| Level 6 · Grade 4 | 5 | "Start when your teacher says 'Go.'" (all 5 speed-reading stories) |
| Level 7 · Grade 4 | 1 | Sports Writing: "Draw and tell **to the teacher**…" |

**Proposed fix:** the pupil sees "Listen as BULIG reads…" and BULIG reads the words aloud. The speaker button already does this on most screens. "Start when your teacher says Go" becomes a big **Go!** button with a timer. Class Demo and the lesson plan keep the module's teacher wording.

## 5. Work that can only be done on paper (55 activities)

These activities show a **"Done with my teacher or on paper"** button, so the app never sees the answer.

| Kind | Where | How many |
|---|---|---|
| Word search, crossword, spiral puzzle, hidden letters, link the letters, match the words | Level 5 · Grades 4, 5, 6 | 20 |
| "Draw…" or "Write it and box/circle it" cards | Level 6 · Grades 4, 5, 6; Level 7 · Grades 1, 2, 4 | 23 |
| Draw/cut pictures, scavenger hunt, write words on paper | Level 2A | 6 |
| Pick a word from a basket, pick a fish from the basin | Level 3 | 5 |
| Post-test card | Level 5 · Grade 6 | 1 |

Examples of the "Draw" cards: "Draw what Samy had", "Draw three boats and color them", "Draw a clock that shows twelve o'clock", "Draw a nipa hut" and "Draw a ring with a single pearl".

**Proposed fix:**
- **Puzzles:** real tap-to-find word searches and fill-in crosswords, using the module's own words, with automatic checking.
- **"Draw…" cards:** the same "Draw or take a photo" tool Level 1 already has, so the teacher sees the work.
- **"Box it / circle it" cards:** tap the word to box or circle it.
- **Basket and fish games:** a tap-to-pick card, like section 4.
