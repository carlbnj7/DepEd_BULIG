Guided tour preview

The first time a pupil or teacher opens their dashboard, their character pops up and asks if they want a quick tour. They can say yes or skip. Each step lights up one part of the screen with a short explanation. A ? button lets them see the tour again anytime. Try it below. Everything is clickable.

Pupil dashboard (phone)

Simple words, big buttons, 7 short steps.

Pupil tour steps
  1. "Want me to show you around?" Yes / Skip
  2. Your name and grade
  3. XP: earn it for every activity
  4. Streak: learn every day, tap to see your calendar
  5. "Let's keep learning" starts your next lesson
  6. Lessons button: all your levels
  7. Calendar, Badges and Me
  8. Done! The ? button shows this again

Teacher dashboard (computer)

A short tour of the main tools in the sidebar.

Teacher tour steps
  1. "Want a quick tour of BULIG?" Yes / Skip
  2. Overview: your class at a glance
  3. Class Demo: teach on the TV, mark as done
  4. My pupils: add pupils and accounts
  5. Manage pupils: starting levels and done in class
  6. Activity history: see answers, give feedback
  7. Teaching guide and My profile
  8. Done! The ? button shows this again

How it would work

  • Shows once, the first time each pupil or teacher signs in (after the welcome animation). It never shows again unless they press ?.
  • Skip closes it right away. A small note says "Tap ? anytime to see the tour."
  • Steps move with Next and Back, or arrow keys. On phones, the page scrolls to each part by itself.
  • Pupils see their own character (boy or girl). Teachers see their male or female teacher character.
  • Only on the pupil and teacher dashboards. Admins and lesson pages do not get it.
  • No database change needed: "tour seen" is remembered for each account.
