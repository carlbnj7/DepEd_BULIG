HOW LEVEL 6 WORKS
  • Each grade has its own Level 6 module; pupils see only their grade.
  • Each story is one activity: first the story card (text, its own picture, key words),
    then one card per question — in the module’s order.
  • Choices, Reality/Fantasy, ordering events (1–5), cause-and-effect matching with lines
    (Grade 1) and “check the conclusion” items follow the module.
  • Speed-reading stories (Grades 4–6) have a Start/Done timer that saves the reading
    time and words per minute.
  • Answer keys and the Grade 2 lesson guides are teacher-only (in each lesson’s Teaching guide).
  • Class Demo → Level 6 → choose a grade to show the cards on the TV.
