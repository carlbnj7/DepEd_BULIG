Meet & Greet
Talking About One’s Self and One’s Family · 45 minutes

By the end of the lesson, pupils can:

  1. Share at least 3 key details about themselves (name, age, favourite activity) in a clear voice.
  2. Talk about 2–3 family members and one thing they like to do together.
  3. Keep eye contact with their audience when speaking.
