Good to know
  • A loading screen appears only while the next page loads. On fast internet it is very short, and it never makes anyone wait longer.
  • Opening screen (A): once each time BULIG starts, not on every page.
  • After signing in (B): once per sign-in.
  • Opening a lesson (C): each time a lesson opens, only if it takes longer than about 0.3 seconds.
  • Pupils, teachers and admins on phone, tablet and PC all get them. Settings that reduce motion on the device make them simple fades.
