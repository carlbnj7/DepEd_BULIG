Good to know
  • "All my levels" is about 50 MB for one pupil (mostly pictures). "My level" is usually 2 to 10 MB.
  • Levels the pupil hasn't unlocked yet are not downloaded. When a new level opens, BULIG offers to download it too.
  • Phones with little storage: if space runs out, BULIG stops and keeps what is already downloaded.
  • Teachers and admins don't get the download offer, because their pages show live class data.
  • With the offline sign-in from the last preview, a pupil can then open BULIG with no internet at all.
