Light, Dark and Auto

BULIG could offer three looks. Light is today's design. Dark uses deep green-black backgrounds with soft cream text and the same gold accents. Auto follows the phone or computer setting, and switches by itself at night if the device does. Try the switch below; the screenshots are real BULIG pages.

How it would work
  • Auto is the default for everyone, so nothing changes for users whose device is in light mode.
  • Each person's choice is remembered on their device and applies right away, with no page reload.
  • Pictures, the green banners and the map keep their colours, just slightly softer at night.
  • Class Demo stays light on purpose, because projectors show light slides more clearly.
  • No database change is needed.
