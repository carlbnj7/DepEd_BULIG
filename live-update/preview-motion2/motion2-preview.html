More animation, effects and design ideas

BULIG now moves between pages, inside activities and in celebrations. These 8 ideas add personality and polish in places that are still plain. Each row shows the effect moment by moment, left to right.

1Sky that follows the time of dayRecommendedPupils

The top of the pupil dashboard changes with the clock: a bright sun and clouds in the morning, a warm orange sun in the afternoon, and a moon with twinkling stars in the evening. The greeting matches the time.

2Gentle "try again"RecommendedPupils
