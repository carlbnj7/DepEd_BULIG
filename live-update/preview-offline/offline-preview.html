Offline mode preview

Pupils save a level while online, answer while offline, and the answers upload by themselves when the internet comes back. Below is what each step looks like on a phone.

1Save a level for offline (while online)

Each level card on My lessons gets a Save for offline button. It downloads that level's lessons and pictures to the phone (about 1 to 24 MB per level). Once saved, it shows a green "Saved on this phone" label.

How it stays safe

  • The pupil must sign in online once on that phone. After that, saved levels open even without internet.
  • Each answer is uploaded only once, even if the connection drops in the middle. Nothing is counted twice.
  • XP, streak days and badges use the time the pupil really answered, so offline days still count for the streak.
  • Activities where BULIG listens to the pupil read aloud need internet to hear them. Offline, the pupil types the answer instead, and the teacher can check the reading in class.
  • On a shared tablet, each pupil's waiting answers stay separate and upload under the right name.
  • Level unlocks, teacher feedback and "Done in class" marks appear after the phone is back online.
