HOW LEVEL 7 WORKS
  • Each grade has its own Level 7 module; pupils see only their grade.
    Grade 1: 46 activities · Grade 2: 43 · Grade 3: 15 · Grade 4: 15 · Grade 5: 15 · Grade 6: 20.
  • Grades 1–2: a story card, then one card per question, in the module’s order.
  • Grades 3–6 (campus journalism): reading cards for the background lessons and articles,
    then the tasks — choices, matching with lines, word-search puzzles, letter boxes,
    sports pictures to name, and writing cards for news, features, editorials and columns.
  • The module text is kept as printed. The copyreading articles keep their errors on
    purpose — finding them is the task.
  • The module has no pre/post tests or answer keys; teachers review the written answers.
  • Class Demo → Level 7 → choose a grade to show the cards on the TV.
