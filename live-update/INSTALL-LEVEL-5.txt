HOW LEVEL 5 WORKS
  • Each grade has its own Level 5 module; pupils see only their grade.
  • Every page is split into cards in the module's order: the story first, then one question per card.
  • Story pages: the poem is text and each picture is cut out on its own with its word under it.
  • Matching: two columns — tap (or drag) a Column A item, then its Column B match, to draw a line.
  • Word searches and crosswords: tap letters to mark the words found; word banks are shown as word chips.
  • Answer keys are never shown to pupils — teachers find them in each lesson's Teaching guide.
  • Class Demo → Level 5 → choose a grade to show the cards on the TV.
