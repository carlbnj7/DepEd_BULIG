More ideas for BULIG

BULIG already teaches, celebrates and works offline. These 6 ideas help pupils see their growth, help teachers spot who needs help at a glance, and bring parents in. None of them repeat what is already built.

1Level certificatesRecommendedPupils · Teachers

When a pupil finishes a level, BULIG makes a certificate with their name, the level, the date and their character. Pupils see it in My achievements, and teachers can print certificates for the whole class at once for recognition day.

3My word bankPupils

Every new word a pupil meets in a lesson (from the module's vocabulary and sight words) is collected in "My words", with its picture from the module. Tap a word to hear it. Words the pupil has said correctly with Speak Answer get a star.

4Daily goalPupils

A small ring on the dashboard: "2 of 3 activities today". When it fills, the character cheers. A week view shows which days the goal was met. The teacher (or pupil) can pick 1, 3 or 5 activities a day.

5Reading speed chartTeachers · Pupils

BULIG already times the read-aloud passages (Levels 4, 6, 7). This turns those times into a simple chart of words per minute, from the pre-test to the post-test, so teachers and pupils can see reading getting faster.

Maria · reading speed+18 words a minute

6Progress report for parentsTeachers

One printable page per pupil for the parent: current level, lessons done, reading speed, badges, the teacher's short note, and a tip to practise at home. Teachers print the whole class at once, for example for the PTA meeting or card day.
